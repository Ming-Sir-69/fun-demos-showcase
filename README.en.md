# Two AI Workflow Showcases

Two curated project copies focused on diagram generation and intelligent Excel translation, presenting problem definition, technical tradeoffs and product-development thinking.

## Projects at a glance

| Project | Focus | Scope of the copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solution Diagram Workflow | Semantic rules, deterministic diagram rendering and reusable Skills | The final Skill and supporting design notes; not a complete mirror of the original project |
| Excel Intelligent Translation | Format protection, local dictionaries and online models, cost and stability | A trimmed design and code showcase; not a directly distributable software package |

The two projects serve different purposes: the former explores how a workflow converges, while the latter presents translation-Demo design and product-development tradeoffs.

## Understanding the copies

- The copies are curated selections from existing projects, rather than all of their original materials.
- The diagram project centers on the rules and rendering approach of `Human Factors Diagram Renderer`.
- The Excel project examines complex formatting, translation paths and tradeoffs in designing a packageable tool.
- This repository provides a showcase and reading material, without a general installation or deployment promise for external users.
